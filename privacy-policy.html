1. Who We Are

Talkyco LLC ("Talkyco," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website Talkyco.live and sells 100% organic, Ayurvedic herbal products including pain relief oils, herbal capsules, syrups, and powders.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit our website, make a purchase, create an account, contact our support team, or sign up for SMS or email communications.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

2.1 Identity and Contact Information

  • First name, last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (when voluntarily provided for SMS opt-in or customer support)
  • Mailing address (for order fulfillment)

2.2 Account Information

  • Username and password (hashed)
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Order history and purchase records

2.3 Transaction and Billing Information

  • Order details, items purchased, and order amounts
  • Billing address
  • Payment method type (e.g., card type, last four digits) — full payment card numbers are processed by our payment processor and are not stored by Talkyco

2.4 SMS Consent Information

  • Phone number provided at SMS opt-in
  • Date and time of consent
  • Opt-in source (e.g., website SMS signup form)
  • Opt-out records (when you reply STOP)

2.5 Communications Information

  • Messages you send to our customer support team
  • Feedback, reviews, or other content you submit

2.6 Technical and Usage Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring URL
  • Cookie identifiers (see Section 7)

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Website forms: Contact forms, account registration forms, order checkout forms, and newsletter sign-up forms
  • SMS opt-in form: When you voluntarily fill out the SMS subscription form on our website and check the consent checkbox
  • Account registration: When you create a Talkyco account
  • Purchases: When you complete a transaction on our website
  • Customer support: When you contact us by email, phone, or through support channels
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Automatically when you browse our website (see Section 7)
  • Third-party services: From payment processors, shipping carriers, and analytics providers in connection with your transactions or use of our services

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill your orders, including shipping and delivery
  • To create and manage your account
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and service-related notifications
  • To respond to your customer support inquiries
  • To send promotional emails if you have subscribed (you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • To send SMS messages if you have opted in (see Section 5)
  • To improve our website, products, and services
  • To detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations
  • To send you information you have specifically requested

Important: We will never use your information in ways that are inconsistent with the purpose for which you provided it, and we will not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. SMS / Text Message Communications

📱 SMS Program Summary

  • Sender: Talkyco (Talkyco LLC)
  • Message types: Promotional offers, product updates, order/service notifications, customer support responses
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to cancel at any time
  • Help: Reply HELP or email hello@talkyco.live

5.1 How We Obtain SMS Consent

We only send marketing or promotional SMS messages to users who have voluntarily opted in by completing our SMS sign-up form and checking the consent checkbox. The consent checkbox is never pre-checked. Providing your phone number for SMS is entirely optional.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or accessing any Talkyco service. You may make purchases, create an account, and use all Talkyco services without providing a phone number for SMS.

5.2 Types of SMS Messages

If you opt in, you may receive:

  • Promotional messages: Discounts, special offers, new product launches, and seasonal promotions
  • Informational messages: Product updates, wellness tips, and educational Ayurvedic content
  • Order and service updates: Order confirmation, shipping status, delivery notifications
  • Customer support: Responses to inquiries you have initiated via SMS
  • Account notifications: Important updates related to your Talkyco account

5.3 How Phone Numbers and SMS Consent Are Used

Phone numbers collected through our SMS opt-in form are used solely to send the SMS communications you have consented to receive. Your phone number and SMS consent information will not be:

  • Sold to any third party
  • Shared with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes
  • Used for purposes other than those described in this policy

5.4 SMS Infrastructure Providers

To deliver SMS messages, we work with telecommunications and SMS infrastructure service providers (such as messaging platform vendors). These providers receive your phone number and message content only as necessary to transmit messages on our behalf. They are contractually obligated to protect this information and may not use it for their own marketing or share it with unaffiliated third parties.

5.5 How to Opt Out of SMS

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any SMS message you receive from us
  • Contacting us at hello@talkyco.live with your phone number and a request to be removed

After you reply STOP, you will receive a final confirmation message and no further promotional SMS messages will be sent. You may re-subscribe at any time by signing up again on our website.

5.6 How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any SMS message or contact us at hello@talkyco.live for assistance with SMS communications.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

6.1 Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party vendors who help us operate our business, including:

  • Payment processors: To securely process your transactions
  • Shipping and logistics providers: To fulfill and deliver your orders
  • SMS / telecommunications providers: To deliver text messages you have opted in to receive
  • Email marketing platforms: To send emails you have subscribed to
  • Website hosting and analytics providers: To operate and improve our website
  • Customer support tools: To manage support tickets and communications

All service providers are contractually required to use your information only for the services they provide to us and to protect your information appropriately.

6.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

6.3 Business Transfers

If Talkyco is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email or a notice on our website if such a transfer occurs and your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6.4 With Your Consent

We may share information for other purposes if you have given your explicit consent.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and understand how visitors use our site.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for core website functionality (e.g., shopping cart, session management). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., pages viewed, time on site). We may use tools such as Google Analytics for this purpose.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences to improve your experience on return visits.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Please refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Account information: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after account closure to comply with legal obligations
  • Order information: Retained for as long as required for tax, legal, and accounting purposes (typically several years)
  • SMS consent records: Retained for the duration of your subscription and for a reasonable period after opt-out to document consent history
  • Customer support records: Retained for the period necessary to resolve your inquiry and for internal quality review

When we no longer need your information, we delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention practices.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These measures include access controls and secure data storage practices.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately at hello@talkyco.live.

10. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information about you. You can also update your account information by logging into your account.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions (e.g., retention required by law)
  • Opt out of SMS: Reply STOP to any SMS message or contact us to be removed from our SMS list
  • Opt out of email marketing: Click the "unsubscribe" link in any marketing email or contact us
  • Data portability: You may request a copy of your information in a structured, machine-readable format where technically feasible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@talkyco.live. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at hello@talkyco.live.

To submit a privacy request, contact us at hello@talkyco.live. We will verify your identity before processing requests.
